1. Research Content
(a) Please describe research problems and key challenges to be addressed in the project
With the deep research of wireless communication technology at home and abroad in recent years,
mobile self-organizing network has begun to develop rapidly. The network consists of mobile
nodes that can communicate freely and does not rely on limited communication infrastructure such
as communication base stations. Among them, the mobile node realizes the network
communication by the cooperation between each other, and a certain mobile node sends the
communication fault in the network does not affect the communication process of the whole
network.
However, in practical application, the topology of mobile ad hoc network changes frequently due
to the movement of nodes, and the connectivity between mobile nodes can not be guaranteed,
which greatly affects the communication performance of the network. In order to solve the
problem that the network communication performance is affected by the frequent interruptions of
mobile nodes, delay tolerance network emerges as the times require.
Opportunistic network is the inheritance and development of mobile ad hoc network and delay
tolerance network, which is composed of a large number of mobile nodes with short distance
communication ability. In the opportunistic network, the mobile nodes distributed in a specific
area communicate with each other through the encounter opportunity brought by their own
movement by carrying Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and other close-range wireless communication devices.
As a bridge between the opportunity network and the mobile social network, the opportunity
mobile social network not only inherits the characteristics of the above traditional opportunity
network, but also has the attributes of the mobile social network. By using the social
characteristics of human beings, such as interests, similarities, daily activities and mobile patterns,
the network realizes message forwarding and data sharing in the network.
At present, researchers at home and abroad have carried out extensive and in-depth research on
zero-information and information-assisted routing algorithms, and achieved certain results.
However, in the opportunistic mobile social network, because mobile nodes have rich social
attributes and huge social relationship networks, how to comprehensively utilize the social
attributes of mobile nodes as well as social relationships and behaviors to design routing
forwarding strategies and improve their routing performance is still a major challenge.

(b) Please state the significance of the project
The opportunistic network adopts ''Store-Carry-Forward'' mode, and the information can be
transmitted from the communication source to the target node in the form of skip forwarding
among mobile users. Therefore, the opportunistic network can realize communication without the
need for full connectivity of the network, which has a significant impact on the realization of
future universal computing. In addition, the opportunistic network has been widely used in remote
area network information transmission, wildlife detection sensor network, emergency rescue in
disaster environment, vehicle network and media sharing.
The working mechanism of data forwarding in opportunistic mobile social network originates
from opportunistic network, which also adopts "Store-Carry-Forward" message forwarding mode.
Mobile social network is a "human" centered mobile communication network, in which the mobile

(c) Please describe research objectives and planned outcomes of the project
At present, researchers at home and abroad have tried to introduce a series of social attributes into
routing forwarding decisions, but it has been found that social relations, which are closely related
to human life, have not been given enough attention. Therefore, the goal of this project is to further
improve the performance of the routing algorithm in the opportunistic mobile social network and
to propose a new routing algorithm based on social and social behavior. In addition, the result of
this project is that the proposed algorithm has good performance in message delivery rate, system
overhead rate and average time delay.

2. Proposed Research Plan and Methodology
(a) Please describe research methods, the theories and strategies adopted in the project
The main research methods of the project are as follows:
（1）In view of the problem that the social attribute which is closely related to human life is not
paid enough attention to the social relations, the project first puts forward an algorithm for
extracting the social relations of the nodes in the social network. According to the characteristics of
human being in social life, this research defines multiple social relations among the mobile nodes in
the network.
（2）After the social relationship among the nodes is extracted, the social behavior among the nodes
is further studied, and the routing algorithm based on the social and social behavior of the nodes is
proposed.
The main theories and strategies used in the project are as follows:
In this project, some classical routing algorithms are mainly used as the theoretical basis.
（1）Epidemic routing algorithm
The famous Epidemic routing algorithm proposed by Vahdat et al is based on a representative
routing protocol. The algorithm makes use of node mobility to increase the connection ability
between nodes, exchange information, and finally transmit the message generated by the source
node to the destination node, and increase the transmission rate by increasing the number of copies
（2）SAW routing algorithm
The single copy routing protocol based on utility reduces the consumption of resources, but brings
great delay. Therefore, a multi-replica routing protocol algorithm with low transmission delay and
small hops can effectively limit the number of replicas, which consists of two stages: the Spray
stage and the Wait stage.
（3）ComPas routing algorithm
This algorithm is a routing protocol based on community model. By studying the social relations
and social behavior between nodes, the group movement model of nodes is extracted, and the data

allocation strategy is proposed, which reduces the allocation cost of message replicas, and finally
obtains higher routing efficiency and stability.

(c) Please describe how you intend to validate your solution/experimental
results/simulations/procedures as well as your implementation plan (e.g., programming languages,
software, hardware, etc.).
Compared with the software imitation is simple and fast, the cost of building the actual network
experiment system is often intolerable. Therefore, most of the researchers use simulation software to
simulate the transmission of information in the real network environment to carry out related
scientific research work. The ONE simulation software can simulate the real opportunity network
environment by simply setting it in its configuration file. Therefore, ONE is used to simulate the
energy saving routing algorithm of opportunity network proposed in this paper.
ONE (Opportunistic Network Environment simulator) was developed by Nokia Research Center
with the support of SINDTN and CATDTN engineering projects. It has the ability to simulate and
analyze a variety of opportunity network protocols, and can realize the integration of mobile mode,
opportunity network routing algorithm and graphical interface. In addition, ONE provides a large
