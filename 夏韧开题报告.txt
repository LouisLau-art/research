1. Research Content
(a) Please describe research problems and key challenges to be addressed in the research
Research Problems
As the demand for communication increases, the transmission of network information gradually
extends to more severe environments, such as interstellar communication, wildlife monitoring, and
mobile social networking. In many cases, the stability between nodes cannot be guaranteed. Without
a stable end-to-end path, the transmission of messages is often random.
In recent studies, scholars have gradually realized that some communication nodes can be divided
by certain characteristics of the nodes to form groups similar to communities. With the introduction
of the node's preference for location, the social relationship between nodes and the introduction of
groups and communities, delay-tolerant networks with social network properties have been
gradually proposed and have quickly become a hot and difficult research topic.
In the Opportunistic networks based on community-aware routing algorithms, nodes in the same
community often have similar characteristics, or nodes have a high probability of encountering.
When messages are transmitted between communities, the community can be regarded as a node.
The entire network is composed of several "community nodes". The community closely connected
to the target community can act as an intermediary for message forwarding, so that messages can be
forwarded quickly.
Based on these characteristics, this article introduces Prophet and Epidemic algorithms in the
messaging within the community. After obtaining the number of nodes in the community, choose
the more appropriate algorithm among the two algorithms.
Key Challenges
1. How to divide the nodes in opportunistic network into different communities rationally.
2. How to get the number of nodes in the community.
3. How to reasonably choose the algorithm of message delivery in the community.

(b) Please state the significance of the research
The communication model of traditional Internet and other networks is based on some assumptions,
such as stable end-to-end connection, two-way symmetrical data transmission rate, relatively short
transmission delay, etc. However, in many application fields in reality, Frequent disconnection of
connections between nodes may be caused by uncontrollable reasons such as node mobility, energy
scheduling, and environmental interference. It is impossible to establish a structured fully connected
network. The traditional multi-hop ad hoc network protocol cannot be used in this challenging
network. Effective operation. However, as a new type of network architecture, the opportunistic
network can better adapt to these challenging environments and meet communication needs. The
opportunistic network adopts a "storage-carry-forward" mechanism, which stores and carries
messages by relay nodes. Until the next chance to meet. At present, more and more application
scenarios are deploying and applying opportunistic networks, such as wildlife monitoring sensor
networks, mobile vehicle-mounted networks, interstellar networks, rural communication networks,
mobile social networks, etc. In social delay tolerance networks, community information is used
Message routing can significantly improve the routing performance of the network. The communitybased social perception routing algorithm first uses a certain community detection algorithm to
allocate nodes to different communities according to the social relationship, common interests,
similarities, etc. between nodes, and then uses the community information of the source node and
the destination node to route select. The message forwarding process is mainly divided into: message

(c) Please describe research objectives and planned outcomes of the research
Research Objectives
The main research object of this article is opportunistic networks. In the social opportunistic
networks, the use of community information for message routing can significantly improve the
routing performance of the network. This paper studies the advantages of different algorithms in
message delivery when the number of nodes is controllable.
Planned Outcomes
On the basis of the opportunistic networks, this project considers the social attributes between nodes,
divides communities according to social attributes, and determines which algorithm to use according
to the number of community nodes. Eventually improve the delivery rate of messages, reduce delay
and message load rate.

Theories and strategies adopted in the research
The main strategy is to flexibly use the two algorithms by combining the characteristics of the
Epidemic routing algorithm and the Prophet routing algorithm to increase the delivery rate.
The main theories used in the project are as follows:
1. Opportunistic networks
The opportunistic networks originated from the early Delay Tolerant Network (DTN). Due to the
instability of network topology and the discontinuity of node connections in this network, it is
divided into several disconnected sub-regions. The disconnection of communication links between
sub-regions, which makes each node adopt the "storage-carry-forward" communication mode.
These nodes generally have storage, communication and computing capabilities for data exchange.
However, in the case of sparse network structure, the data transmission of nodes have a certain
delay.
2. Prophet routing algorithm
The Prophet routing algorithm is a probability-based routing algorithm, which is proposed by
Lindgren et al. Each time the node forwards the message according to the node encounter
probability. Therefore, it limits the number of replicas, in the meantime, increases the delivery ratio.
3. Epidemic routing algorithm
The Epidemic routing algorithm proposed by Vahdat et al. is a representative routing protocol based
on flooding. The algorithm uses the mobility of nodes to increase the connection capacity between
nodes, exchange messages, and finally transmit the messages generated by the source node to the
destination node, and increase the transmission rate by increasing the number of copies. This
algorithm mimics the spread of infectious viruses in biological environments. In opportunistic
networks, when two nodes can connect, they exchange missing messages by exchanging message
vectors. After a period of time, the nodes in the network will receive all the messages.

(b) Please explain the reasons why the methods, strategies, theories stated above are adopted, indicate
the reference to the literature you have read so far and give your comments on the literature.
The reasons for using the above methods, strategies and theories are as follows：
The Epidemic routing algorithm is essentially based on the flooding strategy. Each node stores the
message to be forwarded. The advantage is that no additional topology control information is needed.
At the same time, it can achieve higher message delivery rate and lower end-to-end delay. There is no
need to predict and estimate the state of the link, and it is relatively simple to implement.
The community-based opportunistic network is composed of nodes with similar social attributes and
similar activities. Unlike ordinary opportunistic networks, the social relationship between nodes is
relatively stable and based on community-based opportunistic networks. According to this social
attribute, it has formed different communities. By using relatively active node transmission, it links
to different communities, thus increasing the utilization rate between nodes and enhancing the
relationship between communities.
